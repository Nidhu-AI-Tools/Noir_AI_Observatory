## Argus: A General-Purpose Agentic Runtime for Long-Horizon Reasoning

Researchers presented Argus, an agentic runtime that can persist when evidence supports its current approach and pivot when measurements reveal failure.

**Why it matters:** This research has the potential to revolutionize long-horizon reasoning in AI systems.

[Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05144v1)

## OctoLong: Mid-Training On Cross-Repository Code Contexts Enhances Long-Context Modeling

Researchers introduced OctoLong, a context engineering pipeline that instruments an AST parser and language server backend to facilitate the recursive retrieval of code references.

**Why it matters:** This research has significant implications for long-context modeling in AI systems.

[Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.05141v1)

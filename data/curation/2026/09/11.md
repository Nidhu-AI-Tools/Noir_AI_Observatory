**Why it matters:** Show-Harness enables VLMs to "play" robots through a compact semantic interface linking intent to action.

## Semigroup-JEPA: Latent Dynamics Consistency for Zero-Shot Physics Generalization

Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA) world models learn a compact latent representation of the world that supports prediction and planning.

**Why it matters:** Semigroup-JEPA extends the LeWorldModel framework by supplying the parameter governing the physics to the temporal model via action-conditioning.

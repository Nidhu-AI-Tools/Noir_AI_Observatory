# NVIDIA releases ESM2 language models; research explores foundation model adaptation in medical imaging

NVIDIA published three new ESM2 language models (15B, 3B, and 650M variants) focused on inference and synthetic data. Concurrently, research papers address challenges in adapting foundation models for medical image segmentation and detection, while ecosystem updates include fixes for llama.cpp and LangChain.

## esm2_t48_15B_UR50D · initial-release

NVIDIA published esm2_t48_15B_UR50D. Release kind: initial-release. Version: c82ea31d269aef7c45096ca5d44a1b3cc4b9507d. Availability: downloadable. Categories: language-models.

**Why it matters:** NVIDIA's release of a 15B parameter model indicates continued investment in specialized language models for inference tasks, potentially expanding the utility of their ESM architecture beyond its original protein focus.

[Source](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/esm2_t48_15B_UR50D)

## Medical Image Segmentation: Foundation Model Adaptation versus Task-Specific Training

Foundation models pretrained on large natural-image collections are increasingly adapted for medical image segmentation, in the expectation that they will replace models trained for each task.

**Why it matters:** The study suggests foundation model adaptation is warranted where models must work across sites, with capacity selected on the target domain rather than by the adaptation method.

[Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14519v1)

## Toward Foundation Detection Models via Example-Based Grounding in EM-DETR

DeepLearning (DL)-based Computer-Aided Diagnostic (CAD) systems have shown great promise in supporting clinicians, but their performance heavily depends on the availability of large, well-annotated datasets.

**Why it matters:** The proposed approach integrates diverse medical image datasets to build a multi-domain foundation model for lesion detection, achieving state-of-the-art results by leveraging heterogeneous data.

[Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14518v1)

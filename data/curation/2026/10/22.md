## Joints, Camera, Action! Co-Generating 3D Motion and Video for Arbitrary Rigs

Video generation models can now depict remarkably realistic motion. Yet it is unclear how to use this motion beyond pixels: to animate a specific 3D asset and replay the animation in its 3D scene. Here we present a model that directly co-generates the video and 3D motion for any supplied rig, along with a camera that registers this motion to the scene.

**Why it matters:** This work bridges video generation with actionable 3D motion control for arbitrary rigs, enabling simulation-ready outputs rather than just pixel-based video.

[Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28632v1)

# NVIDIA expands multimodal suite; research advances 3D rendering and reasoning

NVIDIA released Metatron-v1, NLD-Uni, and updated Nemotron models for robotics and media. Research introduced Hexels for efficient 3D reconstruction, ThinkWorld for robot reasoning via world dynamics, and a lattice-gauge theory approach to relational attention. The llama.cpp inference engine added realtime transcription features.

## Metatron-v1 · initial-release

NVIDIA published Metatron-v1. Release kind: initial-release. Version: 27e5f844abd5229f0b7cdbd8df0fab15180be7af. Availability: downloadable. Categories: language-models.

**Why it matters:** Initial release of a multimodal model tagged for inference, robotics, and synthetic data suggests new capabilities in embodied AI tasks.

[Source](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Metatron-v1)

## NLD-Uni · initial-release

NVIDIA published NLD-Uni. Release kind: initial-release. Version: 5db755a2eb0ab84654e3dcf80c7d0245bfe36a50. Availability: downloadable. Categories: language-models.

**Why it matters:** New unified model supporting any-to-any tasks and custom code generation expands the scope of multimodal inference.

[Source](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/NLD-Uni)

## Honeycomb-Surfels: Compact Hexagonal Primitives for Efficient Scene Rendering and Reconstruction

Novel view synthesis and 3D reconstruction have advanced rapidly with radiance fields and splatting, yet their memory and computational demands limit their use in resource-constrained settings. We introduce Hexels: novel planar hexagonal surface elements with a first-order spatial color field for efficient 3D scene and 2D image representation.

**Why it matters:** Hexels improve PSNR over memory-matched 2DGS by 4.53-7.38 dB while requiring lower training VRAM, pushing the quality-efficiency trade-off frontier.

[Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37668v1)

## ThinkWorld: World Imagination Reinforces Chain-of-Dynamics Reasoning

Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models have made substantial progress in robotic manipulation, but their generalization remains constrained by the diversity and coverage of action-labeled robot demonstrations. We introduce ThinkWorld, a VLA learning framework that grounds action reasoning in world dynamics and strengthens it through reinforcement learning with world imagination.

**Why it matters:** ThinkWorld enables human-to-robot transfer and strong generalization to unseen tasks by using unannotated videos for direct supervision via state transitions.

[Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37666v1)

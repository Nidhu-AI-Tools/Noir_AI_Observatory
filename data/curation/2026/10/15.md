# Research and Ecosystem Updates

Recent research papers and GitHub releases highlight advancements in agentic robotics, vision-language-action pretraining, and inference engine development.

## ChunkAct: Action Chunking for Agentic Robotics

Introduces action chunking to agentic robotics, improving task success by 5.0 points and reducing LLM calls, input tokens, and output tokens.

**Why it matters:** Advances agentic robotics, enabling more efficient and effective task completion.

## What Matters in Quality-Aware VLA Pretraining: A Benchmark and Empirical Study

Examines the impact of demonstration quality on VLA pretraining, finding that approach-aware episode conditioning improves mean success.

**Why it matters:** Improves VLA pretraining, enabling better performance after target-task fine-tuning.
